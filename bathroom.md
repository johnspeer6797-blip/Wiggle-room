**Floor edge (base)** ✅
- Matching **3×12 matte bullnose** `SM42P43C9XTMTLW` (Lowe's 1064299), set low as a base, rounded edge up.

**Walls** ✅

- Soap niche: **10 × 10 in**, bottom 20 in above the tub rim, near the controls.

**Tub deck and window ledge**

**Ceiling, dry walls, trim**

- **Blocking** before walls close: grab/towel bar, glass screen, surface medicine cabinet, pedestal sink, tub/quartz support.

## 5. Electrical
- Circuits from the new subpanel: **BATH-R** 20 A receptacle (GFCI) and **BATH-L** 15 A lights/fan.
- 1 GFCI receptacle near the sink + fan/light control. No boxes inside the pocket-door cavity.
- ✅ **Ceiling: run wire now (no smurf tube), close the ceiling, junction box** (John's suggestion, Tisa agreed 2026-11-05).

## 6. Order of work
1. **Check & mark** — mark finished wall faces; test-fill the tub; photograph fan duct; measure sink bolt/supply heights.
2. **Framing** — toilet furring fix (J-01), pocket door (J-05), niches, all blocking. (No medicine-cabinet recess.)
3. **Rough-in** — plumbing (tub drain, sink, toilet supply), electrical (ceiling wired now with junction box), valve depth. No inspection (Tisa: not under permit purview).
4. **Close-in** — cement board + waterproofing in the wet area, drywall elsewhere, ceiling.
5. **Floor** — stock check (J-02), John's dry layout + approval (J-03), set, grout, bullnose base.
6. **Walls** — wall tile, niches, ledge, trim.
7. **Finish** — toilet, sink, faucet, valve trim, glass, accessories, lights, crown, paint, caulk, punch list.
