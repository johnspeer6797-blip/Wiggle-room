# Unit 4 Kitchen — Spec (R1, 2026-11-03)

## Concept
A custom-feeling kitchen with high-end finishes. Deep Essex Green cabinets with concealed panels and brass hardware; a statement tile wall behind the Viking range that runs all the way to the ceiling, with the stainless chimney hood; light, clean counters. The old pie-safe stays and becomes built-in open shelves — a collected, Edwardian touch. The sink sits centered on the window; the counter-depth refrigerator stands on its own on the east wall.
Draft drawn from Tisa's descriptions and mockups (2026-11-07) — Tisa to confirm. Concept mockup: range wall, Oct 26; the floor shown is a placeholder (kitchen floor not chosen yet).

## 7. Order of work
1. **Mark** — tape the cabinet stations on floor and walls (K-M01); mark finished floor height.
2. **Frame** — doorway infill / subpanel wall, backing for cabinets, shelves and hood (J-04).
3. **Mark boxes** — electrician marks boxes against the plan once Tisa locks the layout.
4. **Rough-in** — plumbing to CL 90, gas, electrical, hood duct. **Inspection.**
5. **Close walls**; prime.
6. **Install** — base cabinets, pie-safe shelves, countertop template → install, range-wall tile, floor.
7. **Finish** — sink, faucet, disposal, dishwasher, range, hood, refrigerator, lights, devices, paint, GFCI/AFCI tests, punch list.
